Why Dogs & Owners Love Us

More than daycare. Door-to-door pickup, small play groups, and acres of fenced space to run, sniff, and explore.

Acres to Roam & River Swims

Fully fenced fields, yards, and trails to explore all day. On some days we hike out to the Tolt or Snoqualmie River for swim time — off-leash for pups with good recall, on-leash for those still learning.

Door-to-Door Pickup

We pick your dog up and drop them back home, so daycare fits right into your workday. Pickup and drop-off are included in the daily rate — no extra fee.

Small Play Groups

Never more than 5 daycare dogs a day, so every pup gets real attention and familiar playmates. Some days a few of our own friendly resident dogs join the fun, too.

Free Trial Day

The first day is on us — and it's really about making sure daycare is the right fit for your dog. We see how they settle in, enjoy the group, and play well with others, so you can sign up knowing they truly love it here.

Outdoor Space

A full 2.5 acres of securely fenced yards, fields, and trails, giving every pup room to run, sniff, and explore safely — with space to match calmer dogs with just the right playmates.

Indoor Space

Days are spent mostly outdoors, but when it's time for a breather or the weather turns, dogs come inside to rest on couches and cozy dog beds in our calm lower-level lounge before heading back out to play.

Where We Pick Up

Door-to-door pickup and drop-off around Monroe, Duvall, and Woodinville. Not sure if you're in the zone? Just ask — we're happy to check.

Hello, I'm Rachel

I'm the owner of High Bridge Dog Daycare. Dogs have always been my greatest passion, and over 10 years ago I started getting involved in local dog rescues around the area.

To date I've fostered and homed 60+ dogs, adopting several of those fosters along the way. Moving to a rural property let me open my fenced acreage to even more pups, growing that experience into a dog daycare.

I have a background in pet care and love every dog for their unique personality. You can have confidence that your dog will not only be safe but come home happy under my care.
